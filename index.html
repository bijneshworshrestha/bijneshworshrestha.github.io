Experience

Programme Coordinator

Nepal Republic Media Ltd.

Kathmandu, Nepal Jul 2021 – Jan 2023

Coordinated programme operations at Nepal Republic Media, the company behind Nagarik News and Republica. The role covered 13 programmes across news (English and Nepali), youth and social impact, delivered through YouTube, radio, the Nagarik newspaper, social media and the news website. Worked with eight departments (news, social media, radio, production, marketing, HR, finance and programmes) and reported directly to the Director. The common thread was the system underneath the content: schedules, documentation, reporting, budgets and the people who had to line up for every release.

Operations and systems

  • Tracking system: Proposed and implemented a Google Sheets tracking system and shoot-schedule log. Peers adopted it, and it became the shared reference for what was filming, what was publishing and who was assigned.
  • Daily operations layer: Ran the work that sat beside the programmes: documentation, reporting, communication, and mechanisms that kept processes running smoothly. Each day started with the tracker, the messages and the shoot schedule, and ended with the social media and programme associates updating the sheets.
  • Schedule recovery: When a shoot could not go ahead, checked the schedule for open slots, offered the original guest an earlier date, moved to the second person on the list if needed, and coordinated with the assigned team member so the programme kept its slot.
  • Escalation: Escalated operational risks to the Director and other senior people before they reached the audience.

Programme delivery

  • Daily news output: Coordinated a daily target of six videos plus an end-of-day news programme of ten items, presented by one of two anchors and covering Nepal and the world. Field videos supplied three to six a day, and voxpop, subjective interviews and expert interviews filled the gap.
  • Editorial judgement: Decided which field videos were reportable, based on evidence and substantiated claims, and consulted the editors when unsure.
  • Programme slate: Coordinated Nagarik 360 (investigative journalism across video, the Nagarik newspaper with a QR code, and social media), Sexploration, Spoken Words, Game of Drinks and Nagarik Auto Show, alongside the daily news formats. Six of these ran as continuous digital campaigns promoted across all platforms.
  • Restructuring under pressure: When the old studio was torn down and a new one built mid-run, proposed splitting the partner-produced podcast Sexploration (with YUWA Nepal and Rutgers International) into two seasons with a three-month gap, and evaluated how to make it work. The Director approved, and the show's cost stayed unchanged.

Budget, advertising and revenue

  • Planning input: Evaluated staffing, budget costs and what to prioritise or drop, then recommended decisions to the Director and made the case for them.
  • Advertising packages: Prepared packages with costs and placement spots across the newspaper, website and news and programme videos. Worked out the costs, and the Director approved them.
  • Proposals and contracts: Wrote marketing proposals and drafted advertising and sponsorship contracts. The legal team reviewed and approved them before they went to clients.
  • Reporting to leadership: Prepared monthly KPI reports for HR, campaign progress and slots for Marketing, monthly expenses and estimated budget for further programmes for the CFO, and campaign financial requirements together with the Director.

Campaigns and events

  • Brand Nepal: Coordinated two editions of the annual event, with 50 local brands in each.
  • Nagarik Nayak: Handled coordination, the digital campaign and proposals for the annual award event honouring three social and revolutionary workers, with more than 100 guests.
  • Digital marketing: Oversaw Meta Ads and SEO for the company's own products, and Buffer scheduling, while the social media team handled the technical side. Evaluated performance with the team in Google Analytics.

People and representation

  • Team coordination: Coordinated news team leads, the social media head, the programme associate, the radio editor and production team members, and worked with the heads of the other departments and the people they assigned.
  • Internal workshops: Proposed workshops on teamwork and emotional intelligence in the workplace to HR, then designed and delivered five, capped at ten participants so they stayed productive.
  • External representation: In 2022, represented NRM as a speaker at a workshop on modern radio programmes and the Radio Nagarik revamp, and at an ILO workshop on entrepreneurship in social change and education.
